Arc, take two

Your three objections, answered four ways. All live — drag any of them.

On the handle — your reference already solved this. I pulled frames from the video: that app has no handle at all. It's a giant tick wheel bleeding off the right edge, and the whole wheel is the drag surface. That's the actual lesson: the marker doesn't have to be the target. So in all four below, the entire dial is draggable — grab it anywhere, including the middle. The visible marker shrinks to being an indicator, which frees it to be as small and elegant as it likes.
On color. Two of these run the arc through the same night → dawn → noon fade as the ring, so the arc is the gradient rather than a flat band painted over it. Painted in segments, so it genuinely graduates.
On the arc covering the ramp. Every version here moves the arc inside the ring, so the day/night shading is never obscured. One drops the arc entirely.
