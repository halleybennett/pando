Four ways

Arc inside or outside, ramp full-strength or soft. All four live — drag anywhere.

The marker is now the arc itself. No notch, no bar, no knob — the arc simply ends in a rounded cap that travels with your finger. It's the most subtle marker available, and it only works because the whole dial is the drag target, so nothing has to be aimed at.
The ring got wider and the padding got real. The band is now 38px of ramp with a 26px gap before the hour ticks, instead of the 4px squeeze it had. Numerals sit further out again.
Two gradient strengths. The full ramp is the saturated one from the last round; soft is the earlier gentler version, every stop pulled 46% toward white. Both use the same timing — only the intensity differs, so you can judge saturation without the daylight window changing under you.
Daylight now runs to 21:00. The old ramp was symmetric around noon, which handed half the circle to blue and made the day look short. It's now skewed: cool only from 21:00 to 06:30 — the same window as the sun/moon threshold, so the ring and the city rows finally agree with each other.
