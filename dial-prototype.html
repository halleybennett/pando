Spin it

Three ways to build the same 24-hour ring. Drag each one — they’re live, not pictures.

All three share the approved behavior. A normal drag snaps to 5 minutes. Slow the drag right down and it unlocks single minutes — the label under the dial tells you when you’ve crossed over, which is the visible response that stops the gesture being undiscoverable. Midnight is at the top, noon at the bottom, and the shading is the day/night ramp.
What’s actually being decided here is the thing you didn’t like: how the dial shows where it’s pointing. A hand? A knob? Neither? The ring, the ramp and the readout are the same in all three.
