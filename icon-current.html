Centre root length 26 — shorter means more overhang

Roots: how far the centre one runs

Why shortening the centre root helps at small sizes. Once the two root lines converge the only thing telling them apart is that they are different lengths. The bigger that difference, the longer the eye can still see two levels rather than one bar — so a shorter centre root buys back some of what the tight gap gives away.

The mark in the header, at real size

The header is 16px, and it is unforgiving. The grove's roots are drawn at 1.9 units in a 82-unit box — at 16px tall that is a 0.37px stroke. Sub-pixel. They will not survive, and pretending otherwise would be dishonest, so all three options are shown side by side at the size they actually appear.
