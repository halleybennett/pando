G1, retouched

Your parameters as the base — cutout 0.43, mark 0.69, leaf 13.5 × 34 — with the roots pulled closer, the elbows rounded, and a little more lean.

The elbow is the change that matters most. A vertical stem butted against a horizontal line reads as two pieces; one path turning a corner reads as one thing that grew. The stem and its root are now a single stroke with a rounded turn, exactly as in your sketch — and the centre fork is two rounded turns off one stem rather than a stem crossing a bar.

Pulling the roots together also lengthens the outer stems, since the outer root line drops toward the centre one. That is the same move, which is why it is one slider.
