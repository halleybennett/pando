Icon, round two

Your cutout structure — full-bleed Sky gradient with a white circle punched out of it — against B, and with two readings of the sprig from your second sketch.

Your second sketch is more than a restyle. Three leaves branching off one vertical stem is a sprig — a single connected plant — where the old mark was three separate stalks standing next to each other. That is what stopped it reading as a plant and started it reading as candles. It also happens to be what Pando is: one organism, one root system, many stems.

The cutout is a genuinely different object from the ring. The ring is the wheel quoted literally; the cutout is the gradient with a hole in it. At 60px the cutout reads as a bolder, simpler shape — more colour, less structure — which may be the point.
