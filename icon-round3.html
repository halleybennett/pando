I misread the last sketch and you were right to redraw it. This is the grove kept intact — three leaves at uneven heights, side by side — with each outer stem turning into a lateral root that points at the circle's edge without touching it, and the centre stem running deeper before forking into a root that spreads beneath the other two. Which is Pando: the shared root is the whole organism, and the trees are just what you can see of it.

You were right about the gradient too. I had been filling the cutout tiles with the page tint — #eaeefb → #fbf0d2, which is Sky whitened for use behind text. The wheel is much stronger: it starts at #7c87ce. The tiles now sample the wheel itself, midnight through noon, so the tile and the ring are the same colours.
