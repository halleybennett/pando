All of it, working

Dial, 12/24 and the month picker, live in both treatments. The two phones share one state, so whatever you do to one happens to both — the only difference is the toggle.

Try: drag the dial (slow right down and it drops to single minutes — the ticks turn Klein). Tap 12H / 24H. Open the month control and pick 1 January, then 1 July: Lagos matches Madrid in winter and sits an hour behind in summer, and Auckland swings from +10 to +12. Real timezone rules, not fixed offsets.
