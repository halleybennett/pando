Not a candelabra

You diagnosed it precisely. A candelabra is symmetric, vertical, evenly spaced, with flames on top — and the root line hit all four at once. Each option below breaks at least two, though three of them break it by becoming something else instead.

The root line is the problem, not the idea. Drawing a bar that joins three upright stems is drawing a candle holder, at any size. Three of the alternatives below solve that and immediately become an anchor, a clover, or falling rain — labelled, because a mark that reads as the wrong object is worse than one that reads as nothing. Asymmetry turned out to be the cheap fix: keep the uprights, remove the regularity. Header rows show 12px and 16px.
